# Frontier Command — Content Roadmap

This expands the LATER section of `ROADMAP.md`; it does not outrank the active NOW/NEXT priorities.

## Foundation

- Stable IDs and versioned content files.
- Deterministic mission objectives and trigger conditions.
- Campaign completion state and versioned save migrations.
- Dialogue, briefing, reward, and unlock references.
- Map metadata for spawn zones, objectives, resources, and scripted events.
- Optional terrain metadata for a future Unity client; the current web battlefield remains flat.

## Story mode

- Campaign → act → mission structure.
- Pre-mission briefing and post-mission debrief.
- Primary, secondary, and hidden objectives.
- Mid-mission triggers, reinforcements, discoveries, and branching outcomes.
- Persistent commander upgrades that do not invalidate multiplayer balance.
- Difficulty modifiers separated from canonical mission data.

## Expansion content

- Additional factions with distinct silhouettes and doctrine choices.
- Biomes with different visual and tactical rules.
- Hero/commander units used selectively in campaign play.
- Skirmish map packs and challenge missions.
- Codex entries for units, locations, factions, and recovered intel.

The first campaign stub is `game-content/v1/campaigns/frontier-dawn.json`. Its mission entries are outlines, not promises of final plot, terrain, or mission order.
